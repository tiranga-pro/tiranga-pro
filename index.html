The Symbol of Courage, Unity and National Honor

About Tiranga Pride

The Indian National Flag, proudly called the Tiranga, represents the strength, unity and dignity of India. It is more than a flag — it is the identity of every Indian citizen. The Tiranga stands as a reminder of our freedom, sacrifices and national pride.

Adopted on 22 July 1947, the Tiranga symbolizes courage, peace, prosperity and progress. Every time it waves high in the sky, it inspires millions of hearts across the nation.

Meaning of the Three Colors

Saffron: Represents courage and sacrifice.

White: Symbolizes peace and truth.

Green: Reflects growth and prosperity.

Ashoka Chakra: The 24-spoke wheel represents righteousness and continuous progress.

Why Tiranga Inspires Every Indian

The Tiranga unites people of different religions, languages and cultures. It reminds us that despite diversity, we stand together as one nation.

From Independence Day to international achievements, the Tiranga represents India’s strength and global presence.
